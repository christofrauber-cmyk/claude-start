About

Hi, I'm Christof.

I teach yoga and write in Zurich. My classes are calm, clear and without fuss. You don't need to be flexible, and you don't need to be spiritual. You only need to show up and pay attention.

I bring together the physical practice, mindfulness and what modern psychology knows about the mind. Sometimes that means going against the current. I think that's healthy.

Yoga teaching

Classes for real bodies and busy minds.

The book

Book title Christof Rauber

Yoga beyond the mat.

My book is about the mind: how we build our own cages, and how practice helps us step out of them. It connects yoga and Buddhist psychology with Western science, using everyday scenes like washing the dishes instead of lofty theory.

Mindfulness is not something you add to your life. It is how you live the life you already have.
